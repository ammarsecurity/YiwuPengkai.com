Equipment for testing, monitoring, and adjusting filling-line speed

Handheld tachometers and sensors for reading speed, and variable-speed drives for setting the motors on a water filling line.

  • Balancing
  • Motor testing
  • Chlorine
  • Salinity, TDS, TSS
  • Filling-line speed

Speed is checked with a contact or optical tachometer aimed at a shaft, pulley, or other moving part. When the line needs to run faster or slower, a variable-speed drive changes the motor speed that feeds the conveyor or the filler.

Tell us the motor power, the speed range you need to hold, and whether you are measuring an existing line, adjusting it, or both.

Where it is used

  • Bottled-water and beverage filling lines
  • Speed checks during setup or maintenance
  • Motor-speed adjustment so the filler stays on pace
