Water chlorine level testing equipment

Handheld photometers and colorimeters for free and total chlorine, and DPD comparator kits for a check at the plant or in the field.

  • Balancing
  • Motor testing
  • Chlorine
  • Salinity, TDS, TSS

A photometer reads the sample after a reagent is added and shows free chlorine, total chlorine, or both. A comparator kit does the same job against a color disc, which suits routine checks where a digital meter is not required.

Tell us whether you need free chlorine, total chlorine, or both, the range in milligrams per litre, and whether the instrument stays in a laboratory or travels with the operator.

Where it is used

  • Drinking-water and treatment plants
  • Systems that dose chlorine
  • Bottling plants checking water before filling
