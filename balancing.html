Equipment for balancing mechanical components

Machines that measure unbalance in rotating parts and show where to correct it, from enclosed bench units to larger workshop balancers for heavy rotors.

  • Balancing
  • Motor testing
  • Chlorine
  • Salinity, TDS, TSS
  • Filling-line speed

A balancing machine spins the part, reads the unbalance, and indicates the correction plane. Workshops use that reading when a rotor, fan, pulley, or similar component vibrates or has been repaired.

The photographs include enclosed horizontal machines with a hood and screen, and open workshop machines built for larger, heavier pieces. Tell us the weight and diameter of the part, the running speed, and whether you need a bench machine or a heavy-duty one.

Where it is used

  • Motor, pump, and fan workshops
  • Rotors checked after repair or assembly
  • Components adjusted before they return to service
