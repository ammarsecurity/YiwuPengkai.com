Equipment for testing, inspection, and adjustment of motors

Loaded test benches for running a motor under check, and portable instruments for winding and insulation inspection.

A motor test bench holds the motor, applies a load, and reads the result on a cabinet or a nearby screen. Portable analyzers are used when the motor stays in place and the windings or insulation need a check without a full bench.

Send the motor power, voltage, and whether you need a fixed bench, a portable instrument, or both. Say if the work is repair, incoming inspection, or adjustment after service.

Where it is used

  • Motor repair and rewind shops
  • Inspection before a motor is shipped or installed
  • Adjustment after service
