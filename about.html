An export office for testing equipment

Yiwu Pengkai Import and Export Co., LTD, trading as YiwuPengkai.com, supplies industrial and water-testing equipment from Yiwu, Zhejiang.

Who we are

Based in Yiwu, selling to buyers abroad

The office is at Room 1226, Building A, Fortune Building, No. 988, Chouzhou North Road, Futian Street, Yiwu, Jinhua, China (Zhejiang) Pilot Free Trade Zone. From here we handle inquiries for equipment that workshops, water plants, laboratories, and filling lines use to test, inspect, and adjust their work.

We source the equipment and arrange export. We are not the manufacturer of the machines and instruments shown in the photographs, and the brand names in those photographs belong to their owners.

What we supply

Five equipment lines

An inquiry can name one line or several. The photographs on each page are examples of the kind of equipment we can quote.

  • Equipment for balancing mechanical components
  • Equipment for testing, inspection, and adjustment of motors
  • Water chlorine level testing equipment
  • Laboratory equipment for salt, total dissolved solids, and total suspended solids
  • Equipment for testing, monitoring, and adjusting the speed of water filling lines
